Please analyze this meta-labelling experiment for a trading algorithm.
The goal is to determine if the meta-model adds value by filtering out
low-quality trading signals from the primary model.

================================================================================
1. CONTEXT & SETUP
================================================================================

Asset: GOLD (Gold futures)
Timeframe: MINUTE_15 (15-minute bars)
Label Version: enhanced_neutral
Test Year: 2025 (out-of-sample)
Training Period: All data before 2025

Primary Model:
- Two XGBoost classifiers (Long and Short) predict directional moves
- Each outputs a probability: pred_prob_long and pred_prob_short
- Signal generated when one probability exceeds threshold (e.g., 0.55)
- PnL calculated as log return in signal direction

Meta-Model Objective:
- Binary classification: P(Trade is profitable | Features)
- Target: 1 if pnl > 0, else 0
- Goal: Filter out low-confidence signals to improve overall performance

================================================================================
2. FEATURES USED IN META-MODEL
================================================================================

Feature Descriptions:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
clarity_gap          [Signal Quality]
    abs(pred_prob_long - pred_prob_short): How 'clear' is the signal direction

pred_prob_long       [Signal Quality]
    Primary Long model's probability estimate

pred_prob_short      [Signal Quality]
    Primary Short model's probability estimate

atr_ratio            [Volatility]
    atr_14 / atr_28: Short-term vs long-term volatility ratio

atr_14               [Volatility]
    14-period Average True Range (volatility)

atr_28               [Volatility]
    28-period Average True Range (volatility)

rsi_diff             [Momentum]
    rsi_28 - rsi_14: Momentum divergence indicator

rsi_14               [Momentum]
    14-period Relative Strength Index

rsi_28               [Momentum]
    28-period Relative Strength Index

volume_zscore_10     [Volume]
    Volume z-score over 10 periods (unusual volume detection)

volume_zscore_20     [Volume]
    Volume z-score over 20 periods

volume_roll_mean_10  [Volume]
    Rolling mean of volume over 10 periods

volume_ratio         [Volume]
    Current volume / average volume

hour                 [Time]
    Hour of day (0-23) when signal occurred

trade_direction      [Direction]
    1 for Long signal, -1 for Short signal

adx_14               [Trend]
    14-period Average Directional Index (trend strength)

obv                  [Volume]
    On-Balance Volume

cci_20               [Momentum]
    20-period Commodity Channel Index

sar                  [Trend]
    Parabolic SAR

cmo_14               [Momentum]
    14-period Chande Momentum Oscillator
