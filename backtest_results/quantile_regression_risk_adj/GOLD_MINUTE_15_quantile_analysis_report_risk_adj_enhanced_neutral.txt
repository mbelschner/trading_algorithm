9. COMPARISON: RISK-ADJUSTED VS RAW APPROACH
================================================================================

RATIONALE FOR RISK-ADJUSTED TARGET:
- Raw PnL varies greatly with market volatility
- In high-vol periods: large returns but also large losses
- In low-vol periods: small but potentially more consistent returns
- Risk-adjusted (PnL/ATR) normalizes for volatility regime
- Should produce more stable predictions across market conditions

EXPECTED BENEFITS:
- Better generalization across different volatility regimes
- More consistent feature importance
- Potentially better out-of-sample performance

POTENTIAL DRAWBACKS:
- May filter out high-vol/high-return opportunities
- Correlation with raw PnL may be lower
- Need to track both metrics for evaluation
